The mission is simple: fix the signal, then optimize the page, then prove it in pipeline.

Most B2B landing pages are written for the company, not for the search that brought the visitor there. I map your highest-spend search terms and audiences to pages, rewrite around buyer intent, and run tests you can actually reach significance on.

  • Message match: search-term-to-page mapping so every ad group lands on the right promise.
  • Page briefs & copy: headlines, proof and offers built from customer research, not guesswork.
  • Form strategy: fewer fields where it helps, smarter qualification where it matters.
  • Testing: prioritized A/B test roadmap measured on SQLs, not just form fills.

Ad platforms optimize toward whatever you tell them is a conversion. If that's a form fill, budget flows to the cheapest leads. I connect click to CRM so Google, Microsoft, LinkedIn and Meta can bid toward qualified pipeline.

  • GTM & GA4: clean key events, consent mode and enhanced conversions.
  • Click IDs to CRM: GCLID, MSCLKID and UTMs captured on every lead in HubSpot or Salesforce.
  • Offline conversion imports: MQL, SQL and closed-won sent back to the ad platforms.
  • QA: every tag tested end to end, documented so your team can maintain it.

When GA4, the ad platforms and the CRM all disagree, every meeting becomes an argument about numbers. I build one source of truth from spend to closed-won that leadership and sales both trust.

  • Pipeline dashboards: spend → lead → MQL → SQL → revenue by channel and campaign.
  • Attribution you can explain: clear models, no black boxes.
  • Weekly read-outs: what changed, why, and what to do next.
  • Alerts: automated checks when tracking breaks or lead quality drops.
