The challenge

Google Ads was optimizing to form fills. The cheapest leads were students and vendors, and sales was ignoring paid leads entirely. No click IDs reached [HubSpot / Salesforce], so there was no way to tell the platform which leads mattered.

What I did

  1. Audited GTM, GA4 and the form setup and documented every gap.
  2. Captured GCLID, MSCLKID and UTMs in hidden fields and mapped them to CRM properties.
  3. Set up enhanced conversions and consent mode.
  4. Built offline imports for MQL, SQL and closed-won with values.
  5. Moved bidding to the SQL conversion once volume allowed.

What shipped

  • End-to-end tracking map with QA checklist
  • Automated offline conversion imports
  • Alert when click ID capture drops below [X%]
