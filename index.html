Use cases

Problems I get hired to solve

If one of these sounds familiar, it is usually fixable in weeks, not quarters.

Tracking

"Our CPL is great, but sales says the leads are junk."

Symptom
Cheap leads, low SQL rate, campaigns scaling the wrong audiences.
What I do
Capture GCLID, MSCLKID and UTMs on every lead and import MQL, SQL and closed-won back into the ad platforms.
You get
Bidding that optimizes to pipeline, plus a documented tracking map.
CRO

"Paid traffic lands on the homepage and bounces."

Symptom
High-intent keywords sent to pages that don't answer the search.
What I do
Map top-spend search terms to dedicated pages, rewrite headlines and proof around buyer intent, and set up the first tests.
You get
Message-matched landing pages and a prioritized test roadmap.
Reporting

"Every platform shows a different number."

Symptom
Leadership doesn't trust marketing's numbers; Mondays become reconciliation meetings.
What I do
Define one funnel model, join spend with CRM stages, and build a dashboard with clear definitions.
You get
A spend → SQL → revenue dashboard and a weekly read-out.
CRO

"We redesigned the site and conversions didn't move."

Symptom
Big launches, flat results, no clear reason why.
What I do
Customer and session research, form analysis and a test plan sized to your traffic so results reach significance.
You get
Evidence for every change and wins measured on SQLs.
Tracking

"Conversions dropped after the site migration."

Symptom

What I do
Audit GTM and GA4, rebuild key events, set up enhanced conversions and consent mode, and QA every tag end to end.
You get
Clean data within days and alerts for the next time it breaks.

Channel-level pipeline and revenue you can defend in a budget review.

Process

Data first, then pages, then proof

Testing pages on broken tracking produces confident wrong answers, so the order is fixed.

  1. Diagnose

    Audit tracking, pages and funnel data to find the biggest leak.

    Week 1

  2. Instrument

    Fix tags, store click IDs, import qualified conversions.

    Weeks 2–3

  3. Optimize

    Rebuild key pages and launch tests that move SQLs.

    Weeks 3–6

  4. Report

    Ship the dashboard and a weekly read-out on what changed.

    Ongoing
